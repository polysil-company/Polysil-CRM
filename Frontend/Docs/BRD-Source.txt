Polysil Irrigation — CRM & Dealer Portal: Business Requirement Document
1. Scope of Work
The platform covers two connected systems:
Internal CRM: used by the sales, marketing and management team to manage leads, tasks, schemes, quotes, approvals and reporting across the entire channel hierarchy: Depot → Distributor → Dealer → Sub Dealer → Agent → Institutional Sales → Consumer/Employees.
Dealer / Customer Relationship Portal: a self-service portal for Dealers, Sub Dealers and Agents to place orders, track dispatch, view pricing, make payments, raise complaints and track ratings — plus a direct mobile-number based customer relationship layer for end consumers.
2. Detailed Feature List
2.1 Lead Generation & Marketing
Lead Generation Management: Central lead inbox capturing enquiries from every channel with source, owner, stage and value tracked against each lead.
Auto Lead Capturing (API Based): Automatic lead creation from Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads, your website enquiry forms, Google Ads and other landing pages via API — no manual data entry required.
Marketing Activities: Plan, log and track campaigns, exhibitions, dealer meets and promotional drives, linked back to the leads and sales they generate.
New Leads from Social Media & Other Sources: API-based integration pulls in enquiries from social platforms directly into the CRM lead pipeline in real time.
Lead Priority: Every lead is automatically scored and tagged Hot / Warm / Cold (or High / Medium / Low) based on source quality, enquiry value, response time and engagement level, so the sales team always works the highest-probability leads first instead of a flat, unordered list.
Duplicate Lead Detection: Every new enquiry is automatically checked against existing leads and customers by mobile number, email and name + location. Matching records are flagged for the owner to review and merge, so the same customer contacting through two channels (e.g., a call and a Facebook enquiry) is not tracked as two separate leads.
Cause of Multiple Leads: For any customer who generates more than one lead, the system records and reports the source of each occurrence (e.g., Facebook enquiry → follow-up call → store walk-in). This exposes overlapping marketing spend, repeat enquiries and channel effectiveness at a glance.
Lead Won / Lost Tracking: Every lead that is closed is marked Won or Lost with a mandatory reason (price, competitor, no response, product mismatch, financing not approved, out of area, etc.) plus a free-text note. Management can then see exactly where and why business is being lost, and use it to coach the team and improve conversion.
2.2 Scheme, Territory & Sales Tracking
Scheme Management — State / District / Taluka wise: Create and manage discount and promotional schemes targeted by state, district and taluka, so regional offers can be rolled out and tracked independently across Gujarat and beyond.
3-Way Sales Tracking: Sales are tracked separately across three business lines — Retail / B2C sales, Dealer & Distributor (channel) sales, and Industrial / Institutional sales — each with its own pipeline, targets and reporting.
Instant Quote: Generate a product-wise, GST-calculated quotation in seconds from the CRM and share it with the customer instantly.
Quotation Status Lifecycle: Every quotation moves through a defined, trackable status: Sent → Viewed → Accepted / Rejected / Negotiation / Expired → Follow-up. A complete quotation history is maintained against each customer, making it clear exactly where a deal is stalling and what needs to improve to close it.
2.3 Task, Planning & Reminders
Task Management: Assign, track and close calls, visits, meetings and follow-ups against every lead, dealer or complaint.
Daily Work Planner: Each team member gets a day-wise planner of scheduled calls, visits and meetings so field work is organised in advance.
Reminders & Notifications: Automatic email and SMS/WhatsApp reminders and notifications for pending tasks, follow-ups, payment due dates and scheme expiry — nothing falls through the cracks.
In-Out MOMs: Log Minutes of Meetings for every dealer visit or client meeting, with action items tracked to closure.
Approvals: Structured approval workflow for discounts, scheme extensions and expenses, routed to the right manager with one-click approve/reject.
2.4 Rewards, Gifting & Feedback
Reward System: Track dealer and team performance to automatically qualify participants for reward tiers.
Gifting on Meet Category: Manage gifting for dealer meets and events, categorised by dealer tier (Gold/Silver/Bronze etc.) so the right gift reaches the right category.
Services & Feedback: Capture post-installation service requests and customer feedback/ratings, closing the loop on service quality.
2.5 Subsidy & Farmer Payment Management
Subsidy Management: For every farmer/consumer purchase eligible for a government or company subsidy, the system tracks the subsidy amount, approval status and disbursal separately from the customer's own payment — giving a clear, audit-ready record of who has been paid, what is pending, and what subsidy component is outstanding for each farmer customer.
Farmer Payment Tracking: Full payment ledger per farmer/consumer covering advance, subsidy portion, balance due and final settlement.
2.6 WhatsApp Integration (End-to-End)
WhatsApp Business API Messaging: Official WhatsApp Business API connection used across the entire customer and dealer journey — not just one function.
Lead & Sales Messaging: New lead acknowledgement, quotation sharing, order confirmation and payment reminders sent automatically over WhatsApp.
Complaint & Support Messaging: Complaint registration confirmation, status updates and resolution confirmation sent to the customer/dealer over WhatsApp.
WhatsApp Lead Dashboard: A dedicated dashboard to view, assign and respond to every WhatsApp conversation and lead in one place, tied back to the CRM record.
2.7 Field Sales App, Location Tracking & Team Hierarchy
Location Tracking Sales App: Mobile app for field sales staff with live location tracking, visit check-in/check-out and route history.
Sales Manager App: A separate manager-facing app to monitor the team's daily visits, locations, task completion and performance on a map and in reports.
Hierarchy-Level Team Management: Role-based access mapped to your organisational hierarchy (Management → Regional/Area Manager → Sales Manager → Sales Executive/Sales Boy) so each level sees exactly the data relevant to their team.
2.8 Customer Support Module
Complaints & Tracking: Log, assign and track every complaint from raise to resolution, with SLA timers and status visibility to the customer.
Dealer–Customer–Quality Assessment Connection: A connected workflow linking the Dealer, the end Customer and the internal Quality Assessment team, so a field complaint can be routed to quality assessment and back to the dealer without losing information in between.
Refund Approval Workflow: Formal approval flow for refund requests raised after quality assessment, routed to the appropriate approver before disbursal.
Warranty Tracking: Track warranty status, start/end dates and claim history for every unit sold, linked to the customer and dealer record.
New Change / Replacement Tracking: Track requests for part replacement, upgrades or configuration changes raised by dealers or customers, end-to-end.
Rating System: Dealers and customers can rate service, installation and product quality, feeding into the dealer performance and reward system.
2.9 360° Customer & Channel View
Unified Customer Timeline: A single click on any customer or dealer record opens one consolidated view joining Calls, Visits, WhatsApp conversations, Quotations, Follow-ups, Complaints, Payments, Schemes, Rewards and Ratings for that customer — there is no need to open separate modules to piece the relationship together, even though each activity is still stored and managed in its own module.
Loss / Drop-off Point Identification: Because every interaction is joined chronologically against the customer record, the team can instantly see at exactly which stage — and after how much time — a customer was lost, making it possible to target follow-up and fix the specific step that is causing drop-off.
Improvement Insight: Combined with the quotation history and lead won/lost reasons, the 360° view highlights recurring patterns (e.g., slow follow-up, repeated price objections) so management knows precisely where the team needs to improve.
2.10 Dashboard & Usability
Attractive Dashboard: A single-glance dashboard with lead funnel, pipeline value, task reminders, scheme performance and dealer leaderboard, tailored to each role.
Language Friendly: Interface available in English, Hindi and Gujarati so every team member and dealer, regardless of language comfort, can use the system with ease.
3. Dealer Portal / Customer Relationship Portal
A dedicated portal for Dealers, Sub Dealers and Agents, plus a direct-to-consumer relationship layer:
Sales Order: Dealers raise sales orders directly against live pricing and stock availability.
Material Planning (Stock & Advance Planning): Forward stock and material planning tools so dealers and the depot can plan requirements in advance rather than reacting to shortages.
Dispatch Tracking: Visibility of dispatch status from order confirmation through to delivery.
Pricing & Discounting: Tiered pricing by channel level (Distributor / Dealer / Sub Dealer) with active scheme discounts reflected automatically.
Stock Management: Live stock visibility across depots/warehouses, with low-stock indicators.
Accounts / Payments: Dealer-wise ledger of payments received and pending, with support for NEFT, UPI and cheque records.
Rewards (Gifting): Dealer-side visibility of reward eligibility and gifting status.
Rating System: Dealer performance and service ratings, tied into regular accounting and reconciliation.
Adding Agent: Dealers/Sub Dealers can add and manage their own agents under the correct hierarchy.
Complaints & Tracking: Dealers can raise and track complaints on the customer's behalf, feeding into the same Customer Support module.
Promotional Activities Support: Dealer-facing visibility of running schemes and promotional material to support local selling.
Customer Relationship Portal: Direct mobile-number based lead and company detail capture for end consumers, with marketing/branding content and rating — giving Polysil a direct relationship layer with the end customer, independent of the dealer.
4. Reports & Analytics
The following reports are generated explicitly from the data captured across the CRM, WhatsApp, quotation, payment and complaint modules described above, so performance and gaps are visible without manual compilation:
Lead Conversion Report: Stage-wise funnel from new lead to won, with conversion percentage by source, salesperson and territory.
Salesperson Performance Report: Calls, visits, quotations sent, leads converted and revenue generated per salesperson over a selected period.
Target vs Achievement Report: Planned target versus actual achievement by salesperson, dealer, territory and time period, updated in real time.
Sales Forecast Report: Projected sales based on open pipeline value, quotation stage and historical conversion rates.
Lost Lead Analysis Report: Breakdown of all lost leads by reason code, source, salesperson and territory, to identify where and why business is being lost.
Follow-up Pending / Overdue Report: Live list of all follow-ups, tasks and quotations that are due or overdue, by owner and ageing.
Dealer Performance Report: Order volume, payment behaviour, complaint history and ratings per dealer, used as an input to the reward system.
Territory Performance Report: Sales, scheme utilisation and lead conversion rolled up by state, district and taluka.
Campaign Performance Report: Leads, conversions and revenue generated per marketing campaign or exhibition, measured against campaign cost.
Complaint / SLA Report: Complaint volume, resolution time against SLA, repeat-complaint rate and refund outcomes.

5.1 Change History / Audit Log
Full Audit Trail: Every create, edit, approve, reject and delete action across leads, quotations, schemes, payments, complaints and reports is logged automatically with the user, timestamp, module and the old value → new value.
Accountability: Because every change is attributed to a specific user and time-stamped, any incorrect, unauthorised or disputed change can be traced back and corrected with full accountability.
Read-Only History View: The audit log itself is view-only and cannot be edited or deleted by any role below Management, ensuring the trail cannot be tampered with.
Note: Priority scoring, duplicate detection, won/lost reasons, the 360° customer view and the reports above work together so management can clearly see where the team needs to work harder and where the process needs to improve.
