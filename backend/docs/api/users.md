# API — Users

> Generated by `scripts/generate_api_docs.py`. **Never hand-edit.**
> Base URL: `/api/v1`. Requests need `Authorization: Bearer <access_token>`
> **except** the four endpoints that cannot carry one - `/auth/login`,
> `/auth/otp/verify`, `/auth/refresh` and `/auth/logout` - and anything under
> `/public`.

Conventions for every endpoint in this file:

- Success: `{ "data": ... }`
- Failure: `{ "error": { "code": "...", "message": "..." } }`. Switch on `code`;
  `message` is for a human and may be reworded without notice
- A `422` is schema validation and uses the same envelope, with a `fields`
  map from field path to reason so a form can mark the offending input
- Money is a **decimal string**, never a number: `"1234.56"`
- Timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC; render in IST
- Every POST/PATCH takes `Idempotency-Key`, and replaying returns the first
  response. **The `/auth` mutations are the exception** and take none: login and
  OTP verify are replay-safe through their own ninety-second windows, and refresh
  is rotation, where a repeat is the case the grace window already handles
- Collections accept `page`, `limit`, `sort`, `q` plus the filters listed
- Rows are already scoped by the caller's permissions. An empty list means
  nothing in scope, **not** an error. A `403` means the action is not permitted at all.

## `POST /api/v1/users`

A staff member needs an email, a non-portal role, an open office and a
temporary password of at least 12 characters; tell them the password out of
band. They must change it at first sign-in, and until they do every other call

Indian mobile and an active partner; the role is the partner's type and is

the stored `201`; the same key with a different body (a different password

## `GET /api/v1/users/org-chart`

**Org Chart**

Who reports to whom (FS-080). For any staff member, no permission needed.

By default this is your own picture: the chain of managers above you, nearest
first, and everyone below you, with counts by role. Pass `user_id` to see the
same for someone below you; a `users.view` global holder may pass anyone.
`view=company` gives the whole company, roots being the people with empty
`manager_ids`.

Managers are worked out from office and role: a person reports to the nearest
higher line role in their own office or the offices above it. Nothing is stored,
so the chart follows any change of office or role straight away.

**Parameters**

| Name | In | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `user_id` | query | string \| null |  | Whose picture. Left out: yours. |
| `view` | query | string \| null |  | `company`: everyone, for a `users.view` global holder. Not sent with `user_id`. |

**Responses**

| Status | Body | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `200` | `Envelope_OrgChart_` | Successful Response |
| `401` | `ErrorResponse` | Not signed in. |
| `403` | `ErrorResponse` | `insufficient_permission`: a partner user, or `view=company` without `users.view` at global scope. `consumer_not_allowed`: a farmer's portal account. |
| `404` | `ErrorResponse` | `user_id` is not you or someone below you (and you are not a global `users.view` holder), or the person is deactivated or deleted. On a user's detail page, treat this as 'no reporting line to show'. |
| `422` | `ErrorResponse` | A bad `user_id`, an unknown `view`, or `view` sent with `user_id`. |

## `GET /api/v1/users/setup-check`

**Setup Check**

What is set up wrong, for the admin dashboard (FS-082).

Each check has a count, a one-line title, why it matters, and the first 100
people or partners to fix, sorted by name. Every check is always listed, with
`count` 0 when clean; `ok` is true when all are 0. Read-only.

The checks: field staff in an office that covers no area; staff whose role
works by area but who have none; staff with no manager one level above them;
staff on the System office; partners with no area.

## `POST /api/v1/users/{user_id}/handover`

**Handover**

Hand every open lead this person owns to someone else, for the leaver's last
day. Pick the target from `GET /leads/assignees`. One call moves at most 500
leads and answers `remaining`; repeat with a **new** `Idempotency-Key` until it
is 0 (a replay under the same key returns the stored answer and moves nothing).
`deactivate: true` also signs the leaver out and deactivates them, and is
